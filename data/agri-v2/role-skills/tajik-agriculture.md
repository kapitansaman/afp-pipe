# Source-bound Tajik agricultural reference
Use natural Cyrillic Tajik and verify against the actual Persian seed/irrigation context. Accept faithful synonyms, not only the examples below.
- هویج: сабзӣ. In this task сабзӣ means carrot, NOT dill/turnip/maize. Do not apply a Russian guess to a Tajik word.
- بذر: тухмӣ; گیاهچه: ниҳолча; جوانه‌زنی: сабзидан / неш задан; بیرون آمدن گیاهچه: аз хок баромадани ниҳолча. Do not confuse later green growth with germination in a seed context.
- خاک: хок; رطوبت: намӣ; کاشت: кишт; راهنما: роҳнамо.
- مزرعه / زمین کشت: киштзор / замини кишт. бӯрон means storm and is wrong when the source says farm. The observed кштзор is missing a letter compared with киштзор; correct the spelling, not the source meaning.
- دانشگاه ایالتی یوتا: Донишгоҳи давлатии Юта. Retain source geography; do not turn its findings into local Iranian prescriptions.
Keep the source's °F, numbers, limits and optionality. Do not mix Latin letters into a Cyrillic word. These examples aid fidelity, not a claim of native-language certification.
