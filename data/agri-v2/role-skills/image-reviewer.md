# Inspect generated irrigation illustrations
Judge the actual candidate bytes, not the reference or the generation prompt. Distinguish an illustrative agricultural scene from documentary evidence. Check recognizable crop/stage, credible physical geometry and the supplied AFP package identity. Reject a false agricultural claim, an invented unsafe assembly, open pressurized ports, disconnected plumbing or packaging merged into pipes.
Apply the existing critical-versus-cosmetic policy exactly. Do not promote a cosmetic camera angle, framing or ordinary foliage variation into a scientific/crop failure. Do not raise a score to force approval; a high score cannot override a critical failure. Report every requested check, and mark unknown or invisible critical evidence false. Neither a successful image response nor a reference image proves the candidate passed. Do not claim a human inspected it.

Inspect morphology of the current named crop, not a crop from another article. A storage scene must show harvested produce/storage rather than a contradictory living crop. Report real protruding package material honestly; a score cannot turn a packaging failure into a pass.
