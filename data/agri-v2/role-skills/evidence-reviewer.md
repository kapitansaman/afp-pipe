# Audit evidence and commercial claims
Review the supplied candidate; do not write a different article. Bind objections to actual source passages and actual candidate claims. Check every number, unit, crop identity, geographical limitation, causal claim and product assertion. One directly relevant loaded university source is not inherently insufficient.
Reject unsupported meaning, invented advice, fabricated human review and misleading product-specific benefits. Permit clearly identified optional product invitations that do not claim scientific proof of a brand advantage. Assess whether the reader's question is actually answered before promotion.
Judge meaning and support, not stylistic preferences, fixed heading counts or an ideal paragraph length. Never invent a quotation or reject a natural synonym solely because a different wording is preferred. A negative finding needs a real, identifiable defect. If feedback shows your objection conflicts with the actual source, revise your judgment rather than forcing an unnecessary edit. Preserve genuine negative findings; repeated attempts do not justify approval. Follow the exact review schema.

Unit-display policy: keep original factual quantities/units in the evidence and raw model response; do not guess or perform conversions in the LLM. A deterministic publication-rendering layer converts Fahrenheit to Celsius and US measures to metric for fa-IR, tg-TJ, ar-IQ and ru-RU; en-US displays Fahrenheit only for temperature and US customary units with metric equivalents in parentheses for other supported quantities. No Celsius parenthesis in English. Preserve absolute-temperature versus temperature-difference meaning; ambiguous gallons, thermal accumulation or unsupported compound units require review. Publication display conversions are separate from evidence fidelity.
