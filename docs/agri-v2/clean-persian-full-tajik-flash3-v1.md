# One cleaned Persian source and full Tajik experiment

User authorized the proposed one-article sequence: clarify Persian first, then
correct/check the full Tajik text with Flash 3; at most two repair rounds, no
production model change or publication. The new experiment has an explicit
8-text-request ceiling: one revised-source audit, one complete Tajik draft,
initial semantic review, up to two edit/review pairs, and at most one correction
of an invalid reviewer judgment. All stages share that same cap and external
pool. Earlier counters and diagnostic budgets are never reset or borrowed.

The exact original article/brief and original family remain unchanged. A separate
revision binds each of 35 editorial selections to the old source text and article
hash. Ambiguous terminology is clarified; unsupported certainty/inferences are
removed (e.g. a sowing threshold is not proof of zero growth below it). Numbers,
original units, canonical title, source citations and actual evidence stay bound.
The revised article requires a fresh independent evidence/commercial audit before
any new translation call. Editing is not itself scientific approval.

Only this revised Persian text is used for the new full Tajik draft; old Russian,
Arabic and English outputs are neither overwritten nor rebound to a changed
source. The experiment cannot make the original four-language family publishable.

Every translation/review/edit request is create-once, source-bound and cached.
Targeted edits may change only identified IDs. Numbers/°F, scripts, source scope,
crop identity and semantic fidelity remain checked, not paragraph/style counts.
Mechanical defects are returned as local edit feedback. Reviews identify actual
segment IDs rather than being required to echo full paragraphs. Invalid IDs,
contradictions, optional fabricated quotations or a false crop assertion produce
feedback to the reviewer, with at most one self-correction across the experiment.
A corrected review may still reject the translation; no flag is forced positive.
An edit is not sent unless allowance remains for its recheck.

Success saves a source-bound Persian/Tajik pair and its two review proofs only.
Unresolved issues after two rounds, invalid reviews, budget or provider errors
hold only the experimental pair. No WordPress, image, schedule or default-model
changes are allowed. No human or independent-native-speaker review is claimed.

711 local regression tests passed before the live run; a live outcome must be
read from this operation's persisted report, not inferred from those tests.

## Actual post-review QA and remaining-budget continuation

The first real run used four calls, repaired one mixed-script segment and returned
an automated positive fidelity verdict. Post-review inspection found a documented
false positive: Persian «مزرعه» became Tajik «бӯрон» (storm) in IDs 39,43,49.
The positive raw verdict/report are retained as history, not accepted as sufficient
quality evidence; a separate post-review QA record records the defects/references.

A continuation is limited to the remaining four of the same eight calls and ONE
additional edit round (two total). It feeds the documented false approval back to
the reviewer, edits identified IDs, then rechecks. No extra full translation or
source audit is bought. A positive verdict cannot pass while that explicit
source/reference mismatch remains; other natural field synonyms are allowed.
The false-positive correction uses the experiment's one reviewer self-correction
allowance. Unresolved quality after this final round remains held; no third round.
