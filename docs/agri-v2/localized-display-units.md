# Display units by locale

User-requested policy applies to NEW publication rendering; no retroactive post rewriting.

- en-US: temperature Fahrenheit ONLY (no Celsius parenthesis); other supported measurements use US customary units with metric equivalents in parentheses.
- fa-IR, tg-TJ, ar-IQ and ru-RU: Celsius and metric measurements.
- Research quotations, approved facts and raw translations retain original units/values. Translators preserve source quantities; the deterministic rendering layer, not an LLM guess, performs supported conversions. Existing bound fidelity checks still operate against raw source/target pairs. New rendered titles, excerpts, body and FAQ use localized display values.
- Exact temperature formulas distinguish absolute temperature and explicit differences. Length, mass, area and typed yield/flow ratios use Decimal factors and round to at most three decimals. Ambiguous fractions/comma notation, unqualified gallons, thermal accumulation/rates and unsupported compound quantities require review rather than a fabricated conversion. URL destinations, source files, numbers in URLs, IDs and HTML attributes remain unchanged. This is a finite supported converter, not a claim to cover every possible measurement notation.
- Examples: 68 °F → 20 °C for Persian; 20 °C → 68 °F for English; 1 m → 3.281 ft (1 m) for English; 100 lb/acre → 112.085 kg/ha for metric readers.
