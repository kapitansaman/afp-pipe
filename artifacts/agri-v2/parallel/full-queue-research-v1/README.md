# Full-queue research routing audit

Population: all 2905 immutable new-article topics, not a search sample. Every title and ID was checked for exact uniqueness, all 74 intents and 76 exact crop labels map to explicit contracts in nine research tracks. Queue shards, canonical titles and manifest were not edited.

`topic-research-routing.csv` contains every canonical title, topic ID, exact crop, intent, track, discovery seed count and existing-content review requirement. Source leads are NOT approved evidence or article readiness.

The university source index contains URLs actually fetched and links actually observed on those pages, with timestamp and document SHA-256. Generic hub pages are excluded from final candidate evidence. Secure host/DNS/redirect validation still applies. Source discovery is bounded to 12 total URL fetches and at most two observed-link layers per topic; this is not an unrestricted web-search backend.

Newly extracted candidates carry a server-owned research-route version. Their independent skeptical review must explicitly confirm the route evidence requirements. A calculator needs a supported method, variables and units; diagnosis needs differential checks; feed safety needs exact-species safety evidence. None permits invented numerical inputs or local prescriptions. Existing accepted historical briefs are not automatically rewritten.

One directly relevant university source may satisfy source sufficiency under the owner-approved policy, but single-source reliance is disclosed and no independent corroboration is claimed. Every fact, product identity, unit, geographic limitation, existing-content overlap and later article/image gate remains independent.

A one-shot operation is prepared for up to 20 assigned families, at most four text model calls per family (80 collective), zero image calls, and zero WordPress writes. Workers stop before article drafting, images, translations or publication, and the coordinator independently suppresses publication. Successful prior work and consumed budgets remain unchanged. The general schedule remains OFF.

Offline tests do not demonstrate live model/provider readiness, publication success or an end-to-end 20-family run. See operation/lifecycle receipts for actual live outcomes.
